## 5. Keeping the $530k limit vs resetting to $500k (at 6.34%)

Interest is charged only on what you owe (about $444k) under both options. The limit only changes the **minimum repayment** and how much you can **redraw**.

| | Keep $530k | Reset to $500k |
|---|---|---|
| Amount owed | ~$444k | ~$444k |
| Redraw available | ~$80k | ~$50k |
| Minimum repayment (27 years) | $3,420 | $3,227 |

| Scenario | Monthly payment | Loan paid off in | Total interest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keep $530k, pay the minimum | $3,420 | 18.3 years | **$308k** |
| Reset to $500k, pay the minimum | $3,227 | 20.6 years | $351k |
| Reset to $500k, but pay $3,420 anyway | $3,420 | 18.3 years | **$308k** |
| Keep $530k and redraw all $80k | $3,420 | 26.2 years | $553k |

- The $43k gap comes from the **$194/month higher repayment**, not from the limit.
- The ~$30k of redraw you'd lose with a $500k reset stays paid off, so you keep saving interest on it. You just can't take it back out without applying for a loan increase.
- Every $10k redrawn and not put back costs about **$634/year**.
- **Keep $530k** if you want AMB to force the higher repayment and want the bigger buffer.
- **Reset to $500k** if you want a lower required payment, and set up an automatic extra $194/month to get the same interest result.

## 7. Car loan redraw: options compared

The $18.8k redraw is car repayments you've already paid in advance. It can be borrowed back at 6.45%. It isn't savings.

| Option | Extra interest | What happens to the $18.8k | Car loan ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pay off and close (do nothing) | Baseline | Lost if AMB closes the loan at $0 | ~Aug 2027 |
| Move $18.8k into home loan now | ~$62 over 4–5 years | Kept in the home loan redraw | ~Sep 2030 |
| Move $18.8k into home loan in June 2027, before the final payment | ~$43 over 4 years | Kept in the home loan redraw | ~Jul 2030 |
| Stop car repayments from June 2027 | ~$4 | Car redraw available likely falls $586.27/month (based on September), running out in ~32 months | Balance ~$1,170 left |
| Redraw $600/month car → home from July 2027 (keep the other transfers) | ~$48 | Home redraw doesn't change (the $600 going out on the 7th cancels it). Car redraw falls $600/month: $17.6k (Aug 2027), $11.0k (Jul 2028), $3.8k (Jul 2029), $0 (Feb 2030) | ~2030, with ~$800 to clear |

**Main point:** while the car repayments come from redraw rather than income, the total buffer across both loans shrinks by about $600/month whichever option you pick. The options only change **which loan's redraw** gets used and **how long you keep access** in the meantime.

**Easiest way to keep a backup until ~2030:** change the $600 monthly redraw on the 7th to come from the **car loan (L40)** instead of the home loan (L28), and leave the $586.27 transfer as it is. The car loan stays open, payments are made on time, and the home loan redraw is left alone.

**Must do before August 2027:** the $586.27 transfer runs until further notice. After the July 2027 payment, change its "Credit Account" to L28 or cancel it. Otherwise it will overpay the car loan by about $581 in August 2027.
